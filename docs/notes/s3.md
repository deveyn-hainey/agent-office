## Behaviour decisions
* Seek: waits never block; `waitAnswer` with `auto` auto-answers; routines snap to their end state. Claims are rebuilt from `scenario.agents`.
* After the last event, scenario agents are released to ambient after ~6 s. Agents with a pending ask are never taken by ambient.
* Ambient yields instantly: `director.claim(id)` cancels that agent's ambient activity and calls `agents.stop(id)`. Max 3 concurrent ambient activities.
* Command box: replies `command:result` right after the lead's ack; the act continues in the background and reports with a bubble + log + Slack line, then the request card on `board-4f` is marked `done:true` (extra field; the whiteboard may style it).
  A command that names no agent where one is needed gets a clarification bubble (`ok:false`). "that one / them" uses the last clicked agent (listens to `agent:click` and desk `cam:view`).
* `draw` puts a generic rounded badge with the typed words on `mural-1f` (never artwork).
