# S5 (integration + polish) notes

## Changes
- **Directed camera**: `rt.camera()` (director/runtime.js) is the single gate: honours `ui.routines.camera` and `cam.userActive(20)`. Scenario `camera` events and the new cues use it. `ask` events swing to `desk:<agent>` for ~4.5 s and return. Command box cues: 4F board → all floors while actors travel → the floor where the action is.
- `cam.markUser()` (core/scene.js): UI nav buttons, keys, floor-list clicks and desk open/close count as manual camera use.
- **Routines**: `standups` gates ambient standup bubbles; `camera` gates the director; `daynight` handled by the world. `binder:update` now refreshes the open binder modal.
- **Elevator**: up to 3 agents heading the same way share one car trip (leader holds the doors for walkers, companions board at offset spots); the car is released as soon as everyone is clear of the doors. Elevator is ~30 % faster, doors 0.55 s.
- Clean view keeps the floating nav clear of the model (`ui/controls.js` insets).
- Removed `js/world/debug.js` (unused). Added `scripts/dev-seq.cjs` (dev-only screenshot sequencer), `docs/PRIVACY.md`, `js/director/adapters/README.md`, `.gitignore` entries.

## Known issues
- Floor view is width-limited by the side panels (floor uses ~70 % of the free height); bubbles of agents at the back wall can reach under the top bar, and bubbles near the left edge slide under the left panel.
- Seeking past the end of a scenario leaves the transport at 0:00 and shows no summary card.
- Headless SwiftShader runs at ~6 fps (sim time is clamped to 0.1 s/frame), so live timing checks need `speed=`; a real GPU is much faster (≈290 draw calls, ≈130k triangles in All view).
- `scripts/update-status.py` and `data/{agents,status,activity,floors}.json` are v1 (classic/) leftovers.
